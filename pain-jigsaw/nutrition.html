Nutrition and Lifestyle

Nutrition and lifestyle habits can support your energy, wellbeing and how manageable pain feels day to day.

Why it matters

Living with persistent pain can affect many areas of your life, including energy levels, mood and daily habits.

These factors can also influence how pain is experienced.

A balanced lifestyle can help support both your physical and emotional wellbeing.

Small things can make a difference

There is no single lifestyle change that will remove pain.

However, small changes across different areas can work together to support how you feel.

These small changes can add up over time.

It's all about balance

  • Eat regular, balanced meals
  • Stay well hydrated
  • Aim for a healthy and sustainable routine
  • Avoid extremes or restrictive approaches

A flexible and realistic approach is often more helpful than trying to make large changes all at once.

Everyday habits

  • Maintain a regular daily routine
  • Balance activity with rest
  • Support good sleep habits
  • Make small, gradual changes

Everyday habits can support your energy levels and how manageable your day feels.

Making sustainable changes

Small changes over time are more likely to be helpful than trying to make large changes all at once.

  • Focus on what feels manageable
  • Build new habits gradually
  • Choose changes that fit your lifestyle

Sustainable changes are often easier to maintain and can have a lasting impact.

Be realistic and kind to yourself

It is not always possible to maintain a routine every day, especially during flare-ups or more difficult periods.

This does not mean you have failed — it is a normal part of living with persistent pain.

Returning to small steps when you can is what matters.

A balanced approach

Nutrition and lifestyle are one part of the wider Pain Management Jigsaw.

They work alongside movement, sleep, thoughts and other strategies to support living well with pain.

Using different approaches together can often have a greater impact.
