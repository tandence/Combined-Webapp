Setting Goals that are Important to You

Setting small, meaningful goals can help you build confidence, regain direction and move forward at your own pace.

Why goals matter

Living with persistent pain can make it difficult to stay focused on what is important to you.

Over time, pain can affect your routine, confidence and sense of direction.

Setting goals can help you reconnect with what matters to you and create a sense of purpose and progress.

Small steps lead to progress

Goals work best when they are broken down into small, manageable steps.

This can help build confidence and reduce the risk of setbacks or flare-ups.

  • Start small
  • Build up gradually
  • Recognise and celebrate progress

Setting realistic goals

  • Choose goals that are meaningful to you
  • Make them achievable and flexible
  • Focus on what you can do
  • Adjust goals as needed over time

Goals do not need to be big to be important — small, meaningful steps can lead to lasting change.

What matters to you

When living with persistent pain, it can be helpful to focus on what is meaningful in your life.

This might include relationships, hobbies, work, or everyday activities that give your day purpose.

Goals that connect to your values are more likely to feel worthwhile and motivating.

Working with setbacks

Progress is rarely a straight line. There may be times when things feel more difficult or when symptoms increase.

This does not mean you are going backwards — it is a normal part of living with persistent pain.

Adjusting your goals and continuing at your own pace can help you keep moving forward.

Be kind to yourself

Progress takes time, and some days may feel more difficult than others.

Being patient and compassionate with yourself is an important part of achieving your goals.

Recognising effort, not just outcomes, can help you stay motivated.

A balanced approach

Goal setting works best when combined with other approaches such as pacing, movement and managing thoughts and emotions.

Like the other pieces of the Pain Management Jigsaw, goals are part of a wider approach to living well with pain.
