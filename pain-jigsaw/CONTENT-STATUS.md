# Subpage content status

## Current status

**Not ready for public deployment.**

The visual design and technical page templates are available for web-team
integration. Subpage video embeds and external resource links are still being
worked on and must not be treated as final or approved.

## Before publication

For every video and external resource, the content and clinical owners should
confirm:

- the final URL and visible link wording;
- the source organisation and permission to link or embed;
- that the information is current, clinically appropriate and suitable for
  patients;
- whether the link should open in the same tab or a new tab;
- that linked PDFs and web pages meet the required accessibility standard;
- captions, transcripts and any audio-description requirement for videos;
- video thumbnail, title and description accuracy;
- cookie, consent, privacy and information-governance requirements;
- what should happen if the third-party resource moves or becomes unavailable.

`LINK-REVIEW.md` separately records the draft resource labels that currently
have no destination.
