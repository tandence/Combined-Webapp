# Prepared 12-part demonstration

Use **Open presenter journey** on the demo home page. The links below tell one patient story without requiring the team to watch a full 45-card values sort.

The example person wants to be more involved in ordinary family life, notices that activity can cluster on a better day, and wants a version of gardening that leaves room for connection and recovery.

1. **Open the Pain Jigsaw.** Introduce reliable learning as the front door.
2. **Explore Activity Management.** Show education before asking for behaviour change.
3. **Return to the Jigsaw.** Reinforce the familiar clinical home.
4. **Open Create My Jigsaw.** Move from all available support to a personal picture.
5. **Add clinical and personal pieces.** Use Activity Management, Reconnecting to Life and Gardening as the example.
6. **Choose focus areas.** Narrow attention without discarding the rest of the picture.
7. **Create the connected picture.** Point out the existing save/print outcome.
8. **Explore what matters.** Open the prepared Values example at the goal bridge.
9. **Shape a meaningful goal.** Connect Family to Reconnecting to Life and choose a small first step.
10. **Carry the goal into pacing.** Show that pacing begins with what matters, not a list of restrictions.
11. **Compare overload and balance.** Contrast clustered activity with planned pauses and adaptable versions.
12. **Return to the personal picture.** End with one coherent service story and four standalone entry points.
